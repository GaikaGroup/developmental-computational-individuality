public preregistration of that experiment. Local pre-run records and their
limitations are documented in CHRONOLOGY.md. The original positive architecture
amplification hypothesis was not supported: A=−0.01204, 95% bootstrap CI
[−0.02056, −0.00324], one-sided p=0.99373. All eight behavioral equivalence
comparisons passed. No numerical failures or outcome exclusions occurred.

reanalysis; three checkpoint ZIPs complete the full original raw inventory.
