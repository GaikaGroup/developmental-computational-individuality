# DL-MoE v1.0 hypotheses

* H1 — Blocked early experience increases mature causal specialization versus
  interleaved experience.
* H2 — DL-MoE shows a larger developmental effect than a parameter-comparable
  flat MoE under the same new protocol.
* H3 — Organizational differences persist during a longer common balanced
  phase.
* H4 — Developmental specialization depends systematically, possibly
  non-monotonically, on communication capacity. This is not assumed to be
  monotonic.
* H5 — Organizational divergence can coexist with behavior within the
  predefined equivalence bound.

## Falsification criteria

The claims are weakened if the effect disappears after permutation alignment,
is explained by performance differences, exists only in routing frequencies,
vanishes under causal knockout, disappears during common training, occurs only
for one seed, fails to replicate on unseen seeds, or shows no architecture ×
development interaction. Negative results are valid scientific outcomes.

Knockout identifies causal dependence, not a complete mechanistic algorithm.
P0/P1 are abstract symmetric populations and must not be interpreted as brain
hemispheres or as a model of handedness.
